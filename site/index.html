Less memory

Ten tabs measured 731 MB median PSS, roughly half of Brave 151 and under Firefox 153. Idle tabs hold no live page until you return.

Less noise

A short block list compiles at startup and attaches to every view, so ad and tracker requests are refused before a connection opens.

Less chrome

One native bar carries search, history suggestions, and a six-engine picker. No settings maze and no second start page to manage.

Less leaving your machine

No account and no telemetry. History, bookmarks, and sessions stay in local files under your data home.

Idle tabs hold no live page

Leave a tab alone and br0x releases its web process. The tab stays listed, and one click restores it. Audible, loading, blank, pinned, and just-restored tabs stay exempt.

Tabs sleep before they park

An idle tab first slows its timers and keeps its page. Only under pressure does br0x release the process. Less waking up, less reloading, less memory either way.

Download less browser

One command on Arch, Fedora, Ubuntu and Debian

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SachinD6/br0x/main/scripts/quick-install.sh | sh

It detects your distro, installs what it needs, then builds and registers the app. You answer one sudo prompt and the first build takes a few minutes. Arch users can instead use our pacman repo, and an AUR package follows once AUR registration reopens. Media pages need the GStreamer packages, or the web process exits when audio starts.
